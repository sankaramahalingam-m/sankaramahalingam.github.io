ABOUT ME

Academic leadership with a research-driven mindset.

I am Dr. M. Sankara Mahalingam, an Associate Professor in Computer Science and Information Technology at Kalasalingam Academy of Research and Education, Tamil Nadu, India.

My academic and research journey combines teaching, mentoring, technology innovation and applied research. My doctoral research focuses on blockchain-enabled inter-domain routing for improving reliability, automation and privacy in autonomous systems.

I am interested in developing practical, secure and intelligent solutions that connect emerging technologies with real-world networking and computing challenges.
